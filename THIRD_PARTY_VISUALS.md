# Visual assets and UI sources

Game images are authentic promotional/gameplay images from the official Minecraft website and publisher Steam store pages. Source URLs are retained in public/games/sources.json. All game images, brands and marks remain the property of their respective owners. Images are resized and WebP encoded, not AI-generated.

shadcn/ui: existing Radix-based source components in components/ui.
Magic UI Border Beam: https://magicui.design/r/border-beam.json — new component from the official registry, reviewed and installed manually after the CLI installed its dependency but failed fetching shadcn colors. Source retained in components/magicui/border-beam.tsx.

21st.dev was consulted as design reference; no proprietary or account-gated 21st component was copied or claimed as installed.

The companion package and its download archive were not changed by the design refresh.
